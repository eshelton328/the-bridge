Edit shows handles to reorder workspaces (R7), rename in place, × to close (R6). + is off in edit mode; tap Done, then + creates a chat or a workspace. Dragging a chat's handle onto another workspace moves it (R8); outside edit mode, Move to workspace sits in the chat's actions (M9). Tapping a chat's or workspace's name renames it in place: tapping outside the field saves, and × inside the field undoes the change and closes it.

Proposal. A pane empties when its chat ends or moves to another workspace; the layout does not change, so the other panes stay in place. The empty pane offers a new chat or a chat from this workspace, and Close pane (or Cmd+W) removes it. Close pane in a chat pane's ⋯ menu removes the pane too, and the chat keeps running in the sidebar. The neighbour that shares the longest edge grows into the space (D14).

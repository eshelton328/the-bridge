Proposal. While a chat is dragged over a pane, the half it will take shows as a dotted outline with one line of text, such as “Open drift check to the right”. The pointer’s position picks the place: near the left, right, top or bottom edge splits the pane on that side, and the middle replaces its chat. No target icon shows. Dropping outside a pane, or pressing Esc, cancels the drag.

The iPad gets no split panes: R19 is desktop only, and open question 4 asks whether landscape should get them. Edit shows the handles: drag a workspace to reorder it (R7); tap its name to rename it, × closes it (R6). + is off in edit mode; tap Done first. Dragging a chat's handle onto another workspace moves it (R8). While renaming, tapping outside the field saves and × inside it undoes the change. + opens New chat or New workspace, as on the phone (M15) (R6, R9). The iPad mini uses the phone’s tab bar for now: it shows on top-level pages and hides in a chat (T2–T5).

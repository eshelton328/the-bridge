Proposed lifecycle. A started task moves to Running, at the bottom of the list, and shows its chat’s state. If the chat stops to ask Erik something, the task moves up to Needs you. When the chat finishes, the task moves to Done by itself. The chips filter the list: All, Needs you, Next steps, Running and Done; on the phone they scroll sideways. Under the chips, one row holds search, sort and filter; on the phone, sort and filter are icon buttons.
